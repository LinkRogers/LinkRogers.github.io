### Focus Areas

My day-to-day work lives at the intersection of applied GenAI and enterprise
systems. Below are the themes I'm actively building, writing and thinking about.

#### GenAI for Enterprise Workflows

Retrieval, agents and reasoning pipelines that unlock the value trapped inside
SAP-era business processes. Less hype, more *boring wins at scale*: reliable
extraction, safe automation, measurable decision quality.

#### Production AI / MLOps

What does it take to run an AI system every day without surprises? Observability,
evaluation harnesses, safe rollouts, cost control, and a healthy respect for
failure modes. I care more about the 99th-percentile Tuesday than the launch demo.

#### Industrial Computer Vision

Five years of shipping PyTorch models for perception workloads in real factories
and production lines — detection, segmentation, anomaly and quality. The lessons
from CV translate directly into how I ship GenAI today.

#### Safe & Secure Software

My background in ASPICE-compliant automotive software (FOTA, ECU flashing) still
shapes how I think about secure, verifiable AI systems. "Trust but verify" is
not a slogan, it's a process model.
