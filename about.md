### Hey, I'm Shi Li.

I'm a **Senior AI Scientist at Bosch (China)**, based in Shanghai. My work
sits at the intersection of **Generative AI** and **enterprise systems** —
turning SAP-era business processes into intelligent, automated, decision-driven
pipelines.

### The path so far

My career zig-zagged through three very different domains, and I draw on all
of them every day:

- **Automotive software — SAIC Motor (2018–2020).**
  I built FOTA software following ASPICE (ENG4–ENG8) for secure remote ECU
  reflashing, owned OTA signal design, and shipped virtual dashboard features
  on production bench-test rigs. It's where I learned what *production-grade*
  actually means when the thing you ship lives inside a moving car.

- **Computer Vision — Bosch (2020–2025).**
  As a Senior CV Engineer I led agile delivery of PyTorch-based perception
  models for real-world industrial workloads — detection, segmentation and
  quality inspection. The unglamorous, essential middle layer of factory AI.

- **GenAI & Enterprise AI — Bosch (2025 — now).**
  Today I design LLM agents, retrieval pipelines and decision systems that
  rewire SAP workflows into something fundamentally faster and smarter.
  Production, not prototypes.

### What I care about

AI systems that actually *ship* — secure, observable, boring to operate, and
genuinely useful once they reach the people whose workflows they touch.
The hype is loud; the engineering is quiet.

### Off the clock

I play the **soprano saxophone**. Kenny G famously said,
*"if you do something three hours a day for forty years, you get pretty good
at it."* I try to bring the same patience to both craft and code.
